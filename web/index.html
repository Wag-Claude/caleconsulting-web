FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is AI really useful for a company our size?+

Yes — often more than for large companies, because small teams feel every hour saved. We start with practical, low-cost use cases such as document processing, customer service and reporting.

Do we need to replace our current systems?+

Usually not. We prefer to connect and get more out of the tools you already have, and only recommend new software when the return is clear.

How much does a project cost?+

It depends on the scope. The first consultation is free, and after the diagnosis you receive a written proposal with scope, timeline and cost before any commitment.

Do you work remotely?+

Yes. We work remotely with clients in English, Spanish and Portuguese, and on-site when the project calls for it.
